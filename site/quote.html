Request received

Thank you. Your reference is —

  1. Within four working hours an operations coordinator reviews the request and confirms space on the sailing you asked for.
  2. Same working day you receive a written quotation covering freight, customs, inland transport and all surcharges.
  3. Before you commit we confirm the tariff classification and whether the goods qualify for preference under CETA.
View the sailing schedule Back to the home page

What a quote includes

Ocean freight, terminal handling at both ends, documentation, the customs entry and any inland leg you asked for. Every surcharge is listed by name with what it is for.

How long it holds

Spot quotes are valid for fourteen days. Contract rates are fixed for the agreed term and are not adjusted for peak season.

If you would rather talk

Call the operations desk in Halifax or Rotterdam and you will get somebody who works this lane.
