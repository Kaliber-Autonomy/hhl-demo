Contract allocation

Regular shippers hold guaranteed weekly space, which is what protects you when peak season arrives and spot cargo starts getting rolled. We review the allocation each quarter against what you actually lifted.

Seasonal programmes work differently. A seafood shipper with a heavy autumn and a quiet spring can hold uneven allocation across the year, and we would rather agree that up front than watch you pay for space you were never going to use.
