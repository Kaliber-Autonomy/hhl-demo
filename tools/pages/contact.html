Small print

Policies

This demonstration site collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising technology and no third-party tracking. A single local browser setting remembers that you dismissed the notice, and it never leaves your device. The production site will carry a full privacy policy naming the data controller and the legal basis for each processing purpose.

All shipments will move under a standard bill of lading incorporating the carrier’s terms, including the applicable limitation of liability. Placeholder text is shown here. The final terms will be prepared with maritime counsel in both jurisdictions before the first commercial booking.

This site is built to target level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2. It works with a keyboard alone, respects a reduced-motion preference, and every interactive control carries an accessible name. It has not yet been through an independent audit, so if you find something that does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it.

This site names real organisations because it cites facts published by them. None of them endorses or is connected with this demonstration in any way.

Everything here is a working prototype. Company details, addresses, telephone numbers, rates and shipment records are placeholders. The schedule and the tracking records run on demonstration data. Facts about the ports, the route and the trade rules are researched and sourced on the sources page. No booking made here reaches anyone and no message is transmitted.
