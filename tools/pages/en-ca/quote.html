What a quote includes

Ocean freight, terminal handling at both ends, documentation, the customs entry and any inland leg you asked for. Every surcharge is listed by name with what it is for.

How long it holds

Spot quotes are valid for fourteen days. Contract rates are fixed for the agreed term and are not adjusted for peak season.

If you would rather talk

Call the operations desk in Halifax or Rotterdam and you will get somebody who works this lane.
